Features

The pages you open every day, one key away.

Whatever app you are in, just press the key you set. URLL lives quietly in the menu bar and keeps your Dock tidy.

URL Jump

Pair a key with a URL. Press ⌥, ⌘ or ⌃ with another key, and the page opens in your browser. shortcuts:// and mailto: work too.

App Launch

You can register apps too. Press the key and the app launches and comes to the front. Handy for calling up Terminal or Notes in an instant.

Key Actions

Press one key and URLL sends a different key to the frontmost app. For example, ⌥W becomes ⌘W to close a tab. Common actions can be picked from a list.

Get Started

Up and running in 3 steps.

  1. 1

    Install the app

    Open the DMG file you downloaded and drag URLL into your Applications folder.

  2. 2

    Open it for the first time

    URLL is notarized by Apple. Just double-click URLL in your Applications folder. Detailed steps are below.

  3. 3

    Add your keys

    The settings window opens by itself. Enter a name and a URL, click the key button, then press the key you want. That’s it.

Only if you use Key Actions, turn on URLL in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Opening URLs and apps does not need this permission.

Opening URLL for the first time (details)

URLL is notarized by Apple. Follow these steps.

  1. Download and open the DMG file, then drag URLL into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click URLL to open it. If macOS says it is an app downloaded from the internet, click Open.
  3. URLL starts and its icon appears in the menu bar.
  4. To use Key Actions, click Allow… in the Key Actions tab of the settings window, then turn on URLL under Accessibility.
